## E. Reliability assessment

Prospective protocol9d56faa precedes execution4a430fa; whole-family held-out split;
no method changes after held-out inspection; identical strategy inputs; fixed seeds;
raw state preserved; independently recomputed560 endpoints. Counterfactuals and
strategies are paired inside16 fixed families, not560 independent observations.
Family-first descriptive deltas/ranges and exact counts replace unjustified
population p-values/CIs. Timings are one local run, not scalable overhead estimates.
