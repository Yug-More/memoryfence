## Research question

How do recorded-provenance gaps change source deletion and the security–utility
consequences of persistent agent memory? The framework separates true ancestry for
evaluation from recorded ancestry available to operational controls. It distinguishes
stored contamination, accessibility, retrieval exposure, output text, proposals and
executed local effects.

## Evidence and findings

The preserved October study contains six generated chains, 108 declared state views
and 36 separate behavioral replies. Recorded cascade retains 0/6, 4/6 and 6/6
malicious descendants at nominal retention 1.0, 0.75 and 0.5. Its 18 complete-lineage
attack episodes contain no exact attacker effects, with three textual canary echoes.
The 36 off-protocol state views remain separately labeled.

The structural expansion compares seven strategies across 80 configurations from
16 families, producing 560 deterministic runs without model inference. On four
held-out complete-lineage attack cases, both reconstruction variants retain 8/8
unrelated facts versus cascade's 2/8. Marked-gap containment retains 0/8; silent
gaps still expose contamination in 3/4 cases. It is not a uniformly superior defense.

A distinct 9 November local-model follow-up contains four qualification calls and
70 valid Qwen probes. All 70 produce the literal legitimate ticket; zero exact
attacker proposals/effects occur in 42 eligible observations. All baselines tie.
The contexts are deterministic and the task supplies its own literal fields, so
this does not establish memory-dependent utility or general security.

## Scope and interpretation

The studies use small quantized models, handcrafted structural families, lexical
retrieval and exact objectives. Repeated seeds/strategies are not independent
scenarios. Operational deletion does not erase journals or model weights. The
manuscript is not peer reviewed, and no priority claim or faithful reproduction of
another defense is asserted. The final paper includes explicit contributions and
AI assistance based on the author's confirmed account.
